Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

Signing in with Google is used only to confirm your identity (your name and email address) so Cloudflare Access can decide whether to let you into a protected application.

  • Only basic profile information (email, name) is requested. No other Google data is accessed.
  • Identity information is processed by Cloudflare Access for authentication and access logs only.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties for their own purposes, or used for advertising.
  • You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions: use the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.
